What influences your total cost?

Model and compute

Model choice, tokens or requests, and provisioned compute can affect charges.

Data and monitoring

Storage, networking, telemetry and operational monitoring may add costs.

Support and governance

Support plans, security needs and internal staffing affect total ownership cost.

Azure AI pricing varies by service, region, usage and configuration. Additional costs may include storage, monitoring and support. Use Microsoft's official pricing calculator for a tailored estimate, and review current eligibility, billing and cancellation terms before committing.
